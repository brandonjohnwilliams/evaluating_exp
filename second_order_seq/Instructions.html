Next you will be shown 25 new student profile pairs. This new set of profiles was shown to other participants in this session for their evaluation task 1. That is, other participants guessed which of the two students did better on the economics quiz.

For your evaluation task 2, you are asked to guess which of the two student profiles participants selected for the evaluation 1 task. That is, for each pair, you are asked to guess which of the two students most other participants guessed had the largest number of correct answers on a multiple-choice economics quiz. You will earn $1 each time you select the student profile selected by most participants in evaluation task 1. If participants are evenly split between two profiles, either profile will count as the majority choice.

Please note, for evaluation task 2, you are NOT asked to choose who you think did better. Instead, you are asked to guess which student you think other participants thought did better.

Student profiles are presented side-by-side and the order is randomly determined. That is, you may see a candidate on the left that other participants saw on the right.

Please raise your hand if you have any questions, and we will come to answer you in private. When you are ready to proceed to the comprehension check, click Next.
