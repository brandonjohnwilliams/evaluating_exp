Thank you for agreeing to participate in our study. The other people in this room are also participating, and you must not talk to them or communicate with them in any way. You may not use your phone or any other devices during the study.

Today you will be asked to perform two evaluation tasks and then to complete a brief survey. At the end of the study, you will be paid in private and in cash. Evaluation Tasks

In the evaluation tasks, you will be shown pairs of student profiles side-by-side. You and other participants will be asked to consider which of the two students got a higher number of correct answers on a multiple-choice economics quiz.

Each profile shows the student’s name and first semester transcript with grades received in each course. You select a student profile by clicking on it. You will not be able to choose a profile until 15 seconds have passed, and you will have no more than 2 minutes to decide. Your choice will only count for payment if submitted within 2 minutes.

You will first complete evaluation task 1 and then evaluation task 2. You will be informed of your earnings from the tasks at the end of the experiment.

Details on the evaluation tasks are given immediately before the task. The next page describes evaluation task 1. Please read the description privately and raise your hand if you have any questions. You will be given a comprehension quiz before you can proceed to the evaluation task.
