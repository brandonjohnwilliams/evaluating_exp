You will be shown 25 pairs of student profiles. For each pair you are asked to guess which of the two students had the largest number of correct answers on a multiple-choice economics quiz.

One or more profiles are of real students at the University of Pittsburgh, others are hypothetical. Real profiles are of students who have taken the multiple-choice economics quiz, and who are majoring in or intending to major in economics. Students in each pair have taken similar and equally challenging classes and have a GPA of 3.4 or higher. Course grades are listed highest to lowest. You will not learn which profiles are real and which are hypothetical, but it is in your interest to think of all profiles as having the characteristics of the real students.

After you have completed your evaluations, we will randomly select one pair of real student profiles to count for payment. You will be paid $10 if you have correctly guessed which of the two students had the larger number of correct answers.

Please raise your hand if you have any questions, and we will come to answer you in private. When you are ready to proceed to the comprehension check, click next.
